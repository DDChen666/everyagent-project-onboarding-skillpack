# Risk Checklist

## Risk 0

Read-only inspection and reports.

## Risk 1

Additive control-plane files.

## Risk 2

Docs/adapters/config changes. Requires explicit plan and rollback statement.

## Risk 3

Structural or behavioral changes. Requires explicit approval.

## High-risk areas

- secrets
- deploy
- migrations
- lockfiles
- workflow JSON
- model/prompt presets
- public APIs
- generated files
